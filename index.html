Sharon from Locanto: Safety & Rules

Official Guidelines & Community Safety Directives

Welcome to the official safety guidelines page. Follow these essential protocols to ensure safe and secure interactions.

Important Safety Notice: Always maintain vigilance when communicating or arranging meetings online. Never share sensitive personal or financial credentials.

Safety Rules & Best Practices

  • Protect Personal Data: Keep sensitive personal identity and financial information private.
  • Meet in Public Locations: Always conduct in-person transactions in public, well-lit areas.
  • Verify Before Acting: Double-check all details and listings before proceeding with agreements.
  • Avoid Upfront Payments: Do not send advance payments to unverified parties.
  • Report Suspect Activity: Promptly report suspicious accounts or policy violations.

Community Code of Conduct

  • Maintain respectful and professional communication at all times.
  • Prohibit spam, deceptive listings, and unauthorized promotions.
  • Adhere to all local laws and platform terms of service.
